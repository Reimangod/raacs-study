# Scientific and engineering boundaries

- This is archived simulation evidence, not hardware experiments. No noise
  correction claim follows from correlation-energy Recovery.
- References, orbitals and active spaces are those registered locally. They are
  not all byte-identical replicas of the original papers. Naphthalene uses its
  recorded block-Boys convention; do not call it the paper's exact orbitals.
- Fe2S2 SCI reference quality and some N2 reference-certification details remain
  incomplete. Preserve this uncertainty in Error/Recovery interpretation.
- Matching final shots and raw determinant D does not match initialization,
  construction, pilot cost, spin-adapted dimension, cold setup or classical CPU.
- Fixed CCSD/MP2-initialized LUCJ controls are not necessarily the strongest
  fully VQE-optimized/TN-optimized LUCJ from the literature.
- The historical archive includes exploratory, post-hoc, failed and superseded
  work. Its inclusion is transparency, not additional independent confirmation.
- Uncertified independent parameter counts remain unknown. Coefficient slots
  and zero optimized variables are different quantities.
- CI eigenvectors absent from old records are not reconstructed and relabelled
  as original data. A vector digest alone is not the vector.
- The safe CLI reproduces saved tables/figures and a tiny saved-basis check.
  Fresh full-cohort construction, A100 runtimes and every archived runner have
  not thereby been requalified in a clean cross-platform environment.
- Historical scripts can contain absolute paths, remote host names and side
  effects. They are source snapshots, not safe entrypoints for blind execution.
- Archives exclude installed runtimes/caches and specified duplicate transport
  bundles. See capture-exclusions.json. Native compiled artifacts may remain in
  historical evidence but are not portable executables supplied by the CLI.
- Pattern-based credential scanning is limited; it does not prove that all
  credentials, personal details or third-party redistribution issues are absent.
- Neither a DOI nor hash verification proves scientific correctness or paper
  acceptance. No claim of independent scientific review is made.
